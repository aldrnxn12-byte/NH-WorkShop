Privacy Policy — NH Factory

Effective date: 16 September 2026 · 한국어로 보기

1. Who we are

NH Factory (“the App”) is a Windows desktop application operated by Haneul Bang (방하늘), Naheul Gongbang (나흘공방).

Contact: ace@kkuming.kr · Tel. +82-70-4517-6039

Person responsible for personal data protection: Haneul Bang (ace@kkuming.kr)

2. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to your YouTube channel.

By using the App’s YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google’s Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy

3. Google user data we access and how we use it

  • Scope requested: youtube.upload only.
  • When you click “Upload to YouTube” for a video, the App sends that video file, its thumbnail, and the title, description, tags, privacy/schedule setting and synthetic-content disclosure you reviewed, to YouTube.
  • The App reads the name and ID of the channel you connected in order to show you which channel will receive the upload.
  • The App does not read, modify or delete your other videos, comments, analytics or account data.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and do not use it for advertising.

4. Where data is stored

  • The App has no server. Your OAuth tokens are stored only on your computer in the Windows Credential Manager. Channel name/ID and upload results are stored in a local database on your computer.
  • Locally cached YouTube data (channel name/ID, video IDs) is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.

5. Other services you choose to connect

If you enter your own API keys for AI services (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, ElevenLabs), the text, images or audio you create are sent directly from your computer to those services under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive this data. Keys are stored only in the Windows Credential Manager on your computer.

6. Voice recordings

If you use “My Voice”, recordings and voice models stay on your computer (or, if you choose ElevenLabs, are sent to ElevenLabs under their terms). You may delete them in Settings at any time.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

  • In the App: Settings > YouTube > Disconnect deletes stored tokens and cached YouTube data.
  • In your Google Account: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions
  • Uninstalling the App and deleting its data folder removes all locally stored data.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children.

9. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date.
