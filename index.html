NH Factory

A Windows desktop tool for individual creators to plan, produce and upload their own YouTube Shorts.

What it does

  • Helps you write a script, generate a voice-over and images, and assemble a vertical video on your PC.
  • Lets you review every title, description and setting yourself.
  • Uploads a video to YouTube only when you click “Upload to YouTube” for that video.

Your data stays on your computer. NH Factory has no server that stores your content or Google data.
